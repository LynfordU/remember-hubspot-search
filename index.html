Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

Overview

HubSpot Search Remember is a browser extension that remembers your search and filter inputs on HubSpot so they persist when you navigate back to a page.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Period.

Data Storage

Search text you type into HubSpot is saved locally in your browser's sessionStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is automatically cleared when you close the tab or browser
  • Is not accessible to any website, server, or third party
  • Is only used on app.hubspot.com

Permissions

This extension requests no special browser permissions. It only runs on app.hubspot.com pages.

Third Parties

This extension has no analytics, tracking, advertisements, or third-party integrations of any kind.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the extension's support page.
